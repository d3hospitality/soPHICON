Auth: Sign-in-with-Apple, Google OAuth, and a custom Even-Hub device flow — all resolve to the same auth.users.id.
Realtime: Supabase channels push quest/rank updates to all logged-in clients so the glass and phone never disagree.
Offline: each client keeps a local cache; conflicts resolve last-write-wins on quest status, additive on XP.
