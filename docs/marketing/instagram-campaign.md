# enkiRIDION — Instagram Campaign Playbook (EN)

**Campaign name:** *The Well* — everything flows from Enki's well: quotes, voices,
cards, glass.
**Arc:** Jul 13 (pre-orders) → Aug 8 (launch) → Sep (v1.1 cards/composium re-ignition).
**Companion file:** `instagram-calendar-30day.csv` — importable schedule with
captions + hashtags.

---

## 0. How the Figma file maps to Instagram

| Figma asset | Instagram use |
|---|---|
| 9:16 concept frames (Ask anything, Daily 1-3-5, Seeker to Prophet, Ask a Stoic, 5 minutes, Which philosopher, Real conversation) | **Reels templates, as-is dimensions** (1080×1920) — re-set copy in English |
| Device-gated frames (iOS, Watch, Vision Pro, G2, iPad, Web) | "On Glass" tech-flex reels + launch-week countdown posts |
| Language lanes (9 locales) | The **"same quote, 9 languages" reel** — your multilingual work becomes a flex, not just targeting |
| Philosopher sprites + expression states | Quote cards (crop 4:5 1080×1350 for feed), card-reveal reels, reaction memes |
| Gate pills / E-mark | Brand watermark on every post — consistency = recognition in feed |

There is no EN lane in the Figma file — the calendar CSV carries the English copy.
(An EN-13 lane can be generated back into the Figma file to match the others.)

## 1. Content pillars (rotate these — never two of the same in a row)

1. **Daily Wisdom** — quote cards/carousels. Sprite + quote + one-line modern gloss.
   Zero production cost: Browse mode *is* the content mine (2,801 quotes).
2. **Conversations** — reels of real Speak exchanges. "I asked Epictetus about
   my ex." The product demoing itself.
3. **The Pantheon** — character content: sprite showcases, expression states,
   card reveals, roster teases. Collector energy.
4. **On Glass** — POV device content: G2, Watch, Vision Pro, phone. The
   "this is from the future" wow lane. Best follower-converter.
5. **The Discipline** — Stoicism × gym/warrior culture. Epictetus between sets,
   Stockdale's Enchiridion, "stop whining" energy. Feeds the guerrilla-card
   audiences (gyms, veterans) their digital home.

**Culture-lane reels**

**Engagement-engine reels (the "intelligent" part)**

25. Duet/remix bait: post a philosopher's hot take, invite disagreement. Controversy within taste = reach.

## 3. The comment engine (run this weekly, forever)

The Oracle series (#24) is the campaign's flywheel: every episode generates the
raw material (comments) for the next, trains the audience to return, and every
answer is a product demo. Cadence: bait post Monday, answer reel Thursday.
Rules: never answer mental-health-crisis comments in content — DM a resources
link instead (same bright line as the hospital decision).

## 5. Cadence & timing

- **Pre-order phase (Jul 13–Aug 7):** 1 feed post/day, of which 3–4 reels/week.
  Stories daily (low effort: today's quote + countdown sticker).
- **Launch week (Aug 8–12):** reel every day + countdown stories with the
  pre-order/download link sticker.
- **Times (ET, English/global audience):** quotes 7–8am (wake scroll), reels
  6–8pm (evening peak). Your own dayparting table, applied to yourself.
- Pin three: the launch reel, the Oracle ep.1, the "same quote 9 languages" flex.

## 6. Auto-propagation pipeline (pick one)

1. **Free/native:** Meta Business Suite Planner — schedules IG feed + reels
   ~29 days ahead. Manual entry from the CSV, ~1 sitting per month.
2. **Bulk import:** Buffer or Later — both accept CSV bulk upload; the
   companion CSV's columns (date, time, type, caption, hashtags, asset_ref)
   map directly. ~$15–25/mo.
3. **Power path (you're technical):** Instagram Content Publishing API via
   Make/Zapier/n8n + Google Sheet. Sheet row → scheduled post. The CSV is the
   sheet. This also future-proofs the 9-language expansion: add a `lang`
   column and propagate the same calendar across locale accounts later.

Asset batching: export all frames from Figma in one pass (1080×1920 reels,
1080×1350 feed crops); build 2–3 CapCut/Premiere templates (quote card, screen
capture, POV) so each reel is assembly, not production. One Sunday afternoon
per month of content.

## 7. KPIs (check weekly, adjust monthly)

- Follower→profile-visit→link-tap funnel (link-in-bio → pre-order/App Store).
- Reel completion rate by pillar — kill the weakest pillar monthly, double the strongest.
- Oracle series comment volume (the engine's fuel gauge).
- Saves on quote cards (saves > likes as a wisdom-content signal).
- Post-launch: installs attributed via link-in-bio UTM vs paid campaign CPI.
