# enkiRIDION Reviewer Kit — Even G2 Loaner Guide

A print-and-ship insert for the loaner pair of Even Realities G2 glasses.
Part A goes in the box for the reviewer. Part B is the host checklist you run
between reviewers.

---

## Part A — For the reviewer (goes in the box)

### What's in the box

- Even Realities G2 glasses + charging case (pre-charged)
- This guide
- enkiRIDION philosopher card (QR → App Store / Even Hub)
- Prepaid return shipping label

### 1 · Wake and pair (~5 minutes)

You'll use **your own phone and your own Even Realities account** — the
glasses carry no one else's data.

1. Download the **Even Realities app** (iOS/Android) and create/log in to an
   account.
2. Plug in the charging case, drop both temple arms of the glasses into it —
   the case light coming on means the glasses are awake and discoverable.
   (They ship in a low-power mode; the powered case wakes them.)
3. In the app: **My Devices → [+]** — the app searches for nearby devices.
   Select the G2 and follow the pairing prompts until you see **Connected**.
4. If the app offers a **firmware update, accept it before doing anything
   else** — most connection complaints trace back to stale firmware.
5. Trouble pairing? Forget any "Even G2" entries (both left and right temple)
   in your phone's Bluetooth settings, toggle Bluetooth off/on, and re-add
   from the app. Full walkthrough: support.evenrealities.com → "How to Pair".

### 2 · Get enkiRIDION

1. In the Even app, open **Even Hub** (hub.evenrealities.com) and search
   **enkiRIDION** — "Philosophy on Glass. Think / See / Know." — or scan the
   QR on the enclosed card.
2. Install it to the glasses and open it.

### 3 · The 5-minute experience

1. **Browse.** Let the quotes rotate (every 33 seconds), or scroll a line of
   thought. Watch the philosopher portraits shift expression with the
   emotional weight of each passage — every sprite is hand-crafted pixel art.
2. **Filter by mindstate** — conviction, wonder, serenity, defiance, joy,
   contemplation — and by tradition (Greek, Stoic, Taoist, Confucian,
   Buddhist, Vedanta, Islamic, Epicurean).
3. **Speak.** Ask a question out loud through the G2 microphone and get an
   answer in the voice of the philosopher — try Epictetus on something that's
   actually bothering you. It works in nine languages; it answers in yours.
4. Save any moment with a single tap.

### 4 · Before you ship it onward (~3 minutes — please don't skip)

This wipes your account and data from the glasses for the next reviewer:

1. In the Even app: **My Devices → Glasses Info → Factory Reset**. This
   erases all personal settings and pairing records on the glasses.
2. Then **Remove from Account** (device connection management) so the
   glasses are no longer bound to your Even account — glasses bound to your
   account cannot pair for the next person.
3. In your phone's **system Bluetooth settings, "Forget"** the G2 entries
   (there may be two — one per temple).
4. Charge the case fully, seat the glasses in it, box it with this guide and
   the return label, and ship.

## Part B — Host checklist (between reviewers)

Run this when the kit comes back, before it goes out again:

- [ ] Pair the glasses to your own phone once — this **verifies the previous
      reviewer actually reset them**. If they didn't: do Factory Reset +
      Remove from Account + Forget Bluetooth yourself (you can't ship
      account-locked glasses).
- [ ] Firmware up to date, enkiRIDION current version loads.
- [ ] Unpair/reset again after verifying (Part A §4).
- [ ] Clean lenses and nose pads (microfiber, no solvents).
- [ ] Case charged to 100%; glasses seated.
- [ ] Fresh philosopher card + printed guide + **new prepaid return label**
      in the box.
- [ ] Ship **insured** (this is a ~$600 device) with signature confirmation;
      log tracking number, reviewer name, and dates in the loaner sheet.

### Notes

- **Lenses:** if your spare pair has prescription lenses, say so up front and
  offer the reviewer the demo anyway — the display is readable for most
  mild prescriptions — but a plano (non-prescription) loaner is strongly
  preferred. Reviewers reviewing through someone else's prescription will
  review the blur, not the app.
- **Who gets it:** sequence the kit with your pitch list — the demo that
  can't be conveyed in writing is exactly what the Robertson/Holiday-tier
  pitches need. One reviewer at a time, 7–10 day window each, stated in the
  cover note.
- Support references: Even Support Center — "How to Pair", "Device
  connection management", "User Guide" (support.evenrealities.com).
